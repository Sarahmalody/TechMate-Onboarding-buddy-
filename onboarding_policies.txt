===POLICY_LAPTOP===
LAPTOP FETCHING POLICY — TechMate Inc.

HOW TO GET YOUR LAPTOP:
- Visit the IT Help Desk on Floor 2, Room 204 on your first day.
- Carry your offer letter and government-issued photo ID.
- Submit the Laptop Requisition Form (LRF-01) available at the front desk.
- IT will configure your laptop within 2 hours of form submission.
- You will receive an email at your personal address once the laptop is ready.
- Laptops come pre-installed with: VS Code, Slack, Zoom, Chrome, Microsoft Office.
- For Mac requests, submit a written justification to your manager before day 1.
- Accessories like mouse and keyboard are available on request at IT desk.
- Laptop insurance form must be signed before taking the device.
- Report any hardware issues to it-support@techmate.com within 24 hours.

LAPTOP RETURN POLICY:
- Laptops must be returned within 2 days of last working day.
- Data will be wiped upon return — back up your work to company cloud.

===POLICY_LOGIN===
LOGIN AND SYSTEM ACCESS POLICY — TechMate Inc.

HOW TO GET YOUR LOGIN CREDENTIALS:
- Your corporate email (firstname.lastname@techmate.com) is created by HR on Day 1.
- A temporary password is sent to your personal email ID provided in the joining form.
- You must change your password within 24 hours of first login.
- Password must be 12+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
- For VPN access, download GlobalProtect from the IT portal (it.techmate.com).
- VPN credentials are the same as your corporate login.
- For Jira, Confluence, and GitHub access, raise a request in the IT portal under "Access Requests".
- Access to production systems requires manager approval and is granted after 30 days.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory — set it up using Microsoft Authenticator.
- For login issues, call the IT helpline: 1800-TECH-HELP or email it-support@techmate.com.
- Shared credentials are strictly prohibited and a policy violation.
- Your corporate email can be accessed via outlook.office.com from any device.

===POLICY_LEAVE===
LEAVE POLICY — TechMate Inc.

TYPES OF LEAVE:
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per year. Max 3 consecutive days. No carry forward.
- Sick Leave (SL): 12 days per year. Medical certificate required after 2 days. Carry forward up to 30 days.
- Earned Leave (EL): 18 days per year. Minimum 3 days per application. 7 days advance notice required. Not available during probation.
- Maternity Leave: 182 days paid. Requires 4 weeks advance notice.
- Paternity Leave: 5 days paid. Must be taken within 30 days of child birth.
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family (spouse, children, parents, siblings).
- Comp-Off: Must be used within 30 days of the working day. Cannot be encashed.

APPROVAL:
- 1 to 3 days: Reporting Manager approval.
- 4 to 7 days: Reporting Manager and Department Head.
- 8+ days: Reporting Manager, Department Head, and HR.

IMPORTANT FOR NEW JOINERS:
- Earned leave is not available during your 6-month probation period.
- Casual leave can be availed from Day 1 with manager approval.
- Leave must be applied on the HR portal at hr.techmate.com.

===POLICY_SLANGS===
OFFICE SLANGS AND CULTURE GUIDE — TechMate Inc.

Welcome to TechMate! Here are common slangs and terms you will hear around the office:

TM: Short for TechMate. When someone says "TM culture" they mean the company's work style and values.
True TM: A fully onboarded, culture-fit employee who knows the ropes. Your goal is to become a True TM!
Standup: The daily 15-minute team meeting (usually 9:30 AM) where everyone shares what they did yesterday, what they plan to do today, and any blockers.
Blocker: Something stopping you from completing your work. Always raise blockers in standup early.
LGTM: Looks Good To Me. Used in code reviews or approvals to mean you approve something.
OOO: Out Of Office. You will see this in email auto-replies when someone is on leave.
ETA: Estimated Time of Arrival. Used to ask or tell when something will be done. "What is the ETA on that feature?"
PR: Pull Request. A request to merge your code changes into the main codebase. Needs review and LGTM before merging.
Sprint: A 2-week work cycle in Agile methodology. Each sprint has specific goals and deliverables.
Retro: Short for Retrospective. A meeting at the end of each sprint where the team discusses what went well, what did not, and improvements.
Grooming: Sprint planning session where tasks (called tickets or stories) are discussed, estimated, and assigned.
Ping: To send someone a quick message on Slack. "Ping me when you are free."
EOD: End Of Day. "Please send the report by EOD" means by 6 PM.
EOM: End Of Month. Deadlines that fall at month end.
WFH: Work From Home. TechMate allows 2 WFH days per week after probation.
Async: Asynchronous communication — not in real time. Slack messages, emails, and comments are async.
Sync: Synchronous communication — in real time. Calls and meetings are sync.
Deep Work: Focused, uninterrupted work time. Many teams block 10 AM to 12 PM as deep work hours — avoid meetings or pings during this time.
Onboarding Buddy: Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from the team to help them settle in. Your buddy is your first point of contact for any questions.
Town Hall: A company-wide meeting held every quarter where leadership shares updates on company performance, strategy, and culture.
Kudos: Public recognition given to a colleague for great work. Posted in the #kudos Slack channel.
Ship It: To release or deploy a feature or product. "We are going to ship it by Friday."
Dogfooding: Using your own product internally before releasing to customers.
War Room: An emergency situation where the team gathers (physically or virtually) to fix a critical issue fast.
